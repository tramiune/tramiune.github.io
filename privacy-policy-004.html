Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tramiune (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application AI Photo Enhancer: Unblur & HD (Package ID: com.tramiune004.app.aiphoto, the “App”).

1. Information We Collect

A) Photos and Media Selected by You

  • When you select a photo from your device gallery to enhance, unblur, restore, or stylize, the image is processed solely to generate the requested visual output for you.
  • We do not sell your photos, use your personal photos to train third-party facial recognition databases, or share your private images publicly.
  • Generated or enhanced images saved by you are stored locally on your device.

B) Information Collected Automatically

  • Device or other IDs (such as Google Advertising ID) used for serving ads and measuring ad performance.
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language, app version).
  • App activity and diagnostics (crash logs, ANR reports, and basic analytics events) to improve app stability and user experience.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the AI photo enhancement, restoration, and wallpaper features of the App.
  • To display advertisements and measure ad performance via Google AdMob.
  • To monitor app stability, fix bugs, and prevent abuse or security issues.
  • To review and act upon user-submitted reports of AI-generated content via the in-app report feature.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

We use trusted third-party SDKs that may collect and process limited device identifiers, IP addresses, and diagnostic data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics (Analytics & Diagnostics): https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Your Choices & Data Deletion

  • You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID at any time in your Android device Settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
  • You can delete any enhanced photos or cached data by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling the App.
  • You may contact us via email to request assistance or deletion of any support correspondence.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, to protect information processed by the App.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.
