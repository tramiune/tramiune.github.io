Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Tramiune (“we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use our mobile application LED Banner: Edge Lighting RGB (Package ID: com.tramiune005.app.ledbanner, the “App”).

1. Information We Collect

A) User-Entered Text & Display Customizations

  • Any custom text, emojis, LED banner presets, or RGB Edge Lighting configurations you create in the App are processed and stored locally on your device.
  • We do not upload, store, or share the messages you type into the LED Banner scroller on any external server.

B) Hardware Permissions (Flashlight / Live Wallpaper)

  • The App may optionally use your device’s camera flashlight/torch API (CameraManager.setTorchMode) solely to blink the LED flash when you explicitly enable the Strobe feature in LED Banner mode. The App does not capture photos or record video.
  • The App uses the standard Android Live Wallpaper service (SET_WALLPAPER / BIND_WALLPAPER) when you choose to apply an RGB Edge Lighting or LED Banner theme to your Home or Lock screen.

C) Information Collected Automatically

  • Device or other IDs (such as Google Advertising ID) used for serving ads and measuring ad performance.
  • Device information (OS version, device model, language, app version).
  • App activity and diagnostics (crash logs, ANR reports, and basic analytics events) to improve app stability and user experience.

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, and improve the LED Banner scroller, Concert Fullscreen display, and 60fps RGB Edge Lighting Live Wallpaper features of the App.
  • To display advertisements and measure ad performance via Google AdMob.
  • To monitor app stability, fix bugs, and prevent abuse or security issues.

3. Third-Party Services We Use

We use trusted third-party SDKs that may collect and process limited device identifiers, IP addresses, and diagnostic data in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob (Advertising): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Google Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics (Analytics & Diagnostics): https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

4. Your Choices & Data Deletion

  • You can reset or delete your Google Advertising ID at any time in your Android device Settings (Settings > Google > Ads).
  • You can delete all saved LED Banner templates or cached data by clearing the App’s storage or uninstalling the App.
  • You may contact us via email to request assistance or deletion of any support correspondence.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit, to protect information processed by the App.

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can delete it promptly.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
